It is designed to test your logic! In this simple yet addictive color line puzzle, your goal is to connect dots of matching colours by drawing pipes. Fill the entire board to solve each level—but make sure your lines don't cross! Enjoy thousands of free stages in this offline puzzle game that you can play anywhere without Wi-Fi.

RCBFlow is an independent fork of Flow by sidhant947 (https://github.com/sidhant947/Flow). It keeps the original gameplay and is released under the GPL-3.0 licence. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author.
